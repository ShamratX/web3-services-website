How it works

How we'll work together, step by step

The same four stages whether you need one small contract or a full token launch. You'll always know which stage we're in and what comes next.

  1. 01

    We plan it together

    We talk through what you're building, how the money moves and which chain fits. You get it in writing, with a fixed price and date, before I write any code.

  2. 02

    We build and test it

    We write the code and its tests side by side, put it on a free test network, and show you progress in small updates — no waiting weeks for one big reveal.

    We try to break it

    We attack the code the way a thief would, close anything we find, then trim the transaction fees. Only then does it go live, with the code published for anyone to check.

  4. 04

    You launch, we stay close

    The website goes live, we walk you through your control panel, and we're reachable through launch week in case anything needs a quick hand.

FAQ

Questions people ask us first

If yours isn't here, just email us — we answer these all day and we don't mind at all.

I'm not technical. Can you still help me?

Yes, and a good share of our clients are in exactly that position. You don't need to know what a smart contract is — you just need to know what you want your project to do. We'll explain the options in plain language, recommend the sensible one, and handle everything technical from there.

Can you build both the contracts and the website?

Yes, and it's what most people ask for. You get the contracts and the website that connects to them — wallet sign-in, buy and claim buttons, and a control panel for your team. Because one team builds both halves, there's no gap where two agencies blame each other.

How long will my project take?

A straightforward token, tested and live, is usually 3 to 5 days. A complete presale platform with a dashboard, lock-up schedules and claims runs 2 to 4 weeks depending on how much it needs to do. We'll give you a firm date before we start, not a vague range.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you're building, so we quote per project rather than posting a price list that would mislead you. Send us a short description and you'll get a real number, not a "contact us for pricing" runaround. Payment is split into stages, so you're never far out of pocket.

How do I know the code is safe?

Every contract comes with a full set of automated tests, and we spend time deliberately attacking our own code — trying to drain it, break its limits or get around its permissions. The finished code is published on the blockchain explorer so anyone can read it, and we can work alongside an outside auditor if your launch needs that.

Do I own the code?

All of it. The code, the contracts and the keys are yours, delivered into your own repository with written notes. Nothing is locked to us, and any developer can pick it up after we're done.

Can you rescue a project someone else started?

Often, yes — it's a regular request. We'll read through what exists, tell you honestly what's usable and what isn't, then fix, finish or rebuild the parts that need it. You'll get that assessment before you commit to the work.

Which blockchain should I choose?

Tell us who your users are and what your budget is, and we'll recommend one. As a rough guide: BNB Chain for a cheap, fast token launch, Ethereum when trust matters most, Polygon or Base when you want low fees for lots of everyday users. Adding a second chain later is straightforward.
